How Navigation Layouts Work

A well-designed navigation layout guides readers from the global site chrome into the primary content without competing with it. Regional landmarks such as header, nav, aside and footer help assistive technology users skip directly to the main region.

The sidebar alongside this article demonstrates supplementary content that is related but not essential. Readers can safely ignore it without losing the substance of the article itself.

Designers should keep boilerplate regions visually distinct from the content column, because screen reader users depend on consistent landmark roles to navigate efficiently through long pages.
