List Examples

Lists structure related information into scannable groups. This page demonstrates unordered lists, ordered lists, and deeply nested combinations of both used throughout real documentation.

Supported Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
    • Granulated sugar
    • Brown sugar
      • Light brown
      • Dark brown
    • Powdered sugar
  • Butter

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven
  2. Mix dry ingredients
    1. Sift the flour
    2. Whisk the sugar
  3. Bake until golden

Following the steps in order yields a consistent result every time, provided the ingredients are fresh and the oven is properly calibrated before baking begins.
